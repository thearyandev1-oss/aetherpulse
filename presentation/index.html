Dynamic AI-Driven Commuter Handoffs & Traffic Simulation

The Problem

  • Static traffic lights cause massive urban congestion.
  • Commuters face disjointed "last-mile" travel.
  • Wasted time, increased CO₂ emissions, and high fuel costs.

Our Solution

  • Live Digital Twin: AI monitors intersections in real-time.
  • EADSO Algorithm: Dynamically optimizes traffic light timers.
  • Multi-Modal Handoff: Seamlessly routes users combining trains, buses, and smart cabs.

Real-World Impact (vs Normal Cabs)

  • 31% Lower CO₂ Emissions via optimized routing
  • ₹120-180 Saved per average commuter trip
